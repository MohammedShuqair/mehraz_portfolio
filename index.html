About

Research, policy, and community work

Mehraz is a research professional with more than five years of experience in social research, policy analysis, and community development. He designs and leads equity-focused research projects, manages teams and budgets, and translates findings into practical policy and community action.

His work spans government, Indigenous organizations, academic institutions, and frontline community programs. He brings an anti-racist, trauma-informed, and decolonial approach to research and service design, with a focus on lifelong learning, mentorship, and transformative social change.
